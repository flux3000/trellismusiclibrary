Icon Pass 1

Every Unicode character app.js currently uses as an icon, with counts from the live file. The proposal is deliberately not "replace all of them" — a glyph sitting in front of a word is decoration, and swapping it for an SVG keeps the decoration while making it look more designed. Three verdicts below. Lucide v1.33.0, ISC.

Replace — a glyph standing alone as a control

These are buttons whose entire visible content is one character. There is nothing else carrying the meaning, so an icon is doing real work rather than ornamenting a label. This is where Lucide earns its place.

Drop — a glyph pinned to the front of a word

Here the label already says it. ✓ Match is not clearer than Match; the colour is doing the work and the tick is a second, weaker copy of the same signal. Replacing these with SVGs would keep the ornament and make it look more deliberate, which is the opposite of what we want. My recommendation is to delete the character and change nothing else.
