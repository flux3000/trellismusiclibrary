What A fixes

  • Performers grid → gone. One linear list, sorted A–Z by default, Newest / Oldest / Recently added one click away. This is the layout you asked for and the one collectors expect.
  • "Recommended" → "Random Top Shows", six across instead of three, each tile ~40% the height of today's handbill. The refresh reads as Shuffle with a rotate icon — a refresh affordance, not "show me three more".
  • On This Day promoted to the top as a single 42px band with full dates, and it is narrow by construction — one line, horizontally scrolling, "See all" for the rest.
  • Genre browse as colour chips with counts. Cheap, and it is the one dimension where your data is nearly complete (566/580).
  • Collections get colour: each card is striped with the genre colours of what it holds, so four collections still look intentional rather than sparse.
  • Vibrance comes from the genre spine on every row plus the photo ring on the avatar — colour on all 580 rows, not just the 312 with faces.

What B does differently

  • Images lead. Four tiles at 150px tall, photo with a scrim so the type stays readable. This is the most vibrant option and it depends on the 54% — it will look best on Esbjörn Svensson, Tony Rice, Joni Mitchell, and plainest on the long tail.
  • Genre as full-width bands rather than chips — heavier presence, reads as a way in rather than a filter.
  • Decade grouping on the list. Your spread supports it: 2000s 158, 1990s 125, 1970s 105, 1960s 76. A–Z stays available.
  • On This Day in a 300px right rail — the direct fix for "way too wide", and it stops being buried without stealing the top of the page.
  • Cost: more vertical space before the list starts, and it leans on photo coverage you would want to keep improving.

My recommendation

A, with B's hero band as an option later. A fixes every complaint using data you have complete coverage for — genre colour on 566 of 580 — and it puts the linear list first, which is what you actually asked for and what a collector opens the page to do.

B is the better-looking page and the weaker product right now. Its hero band is the first thing you see, and for 46% of the library it degrades to a coloured rectangle. That is a fine fallback in a six-across strip where a few plain tiles sit among photographed ones; it is more exposed at 150px, four across, above the fold.

If photo coverage climbs — the Performer page already has the Commons fetch — B's hero band drops into A without disturbing anything else. Worth treating as a content project with its own payoff rather than a design blocker.

Two things I need you to decide

  • "Random Top Shows" — what qualifies? Today's Recommended is A/A+ only, weighted toward unplayed, one per performer, seeded stable-per-day. Only 118 recordings have a letter grade at all, so "top" currently means "one of ~118 graded shows". Either keep that and accept the pool, or widen it to include the automated listening-quality score.
  • Default sort on the list. A–Z by performer, or chronological by show date? A–Z is easier to aim at when you know what you want; chronological reads like a collection. I have drawn A default in A and chronological in B.
